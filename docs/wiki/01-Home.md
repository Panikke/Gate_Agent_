# Morning Wave

Morning Wave is a planning puzzle set on an airport apron between 06:30 and 12:00. A wave of flights is due out. Each needs a stand for the aircraft, a gate for the passengers and check-in desks to process them. There are not enough of any of these to go round, so some flights have to wait. Your job is to decide who waits, for how long, and where everyone goes.

When your plan is ready you press **Run the wave** and watch the morning play out on the departures board. Then you get a score, a medal if you earned one, and the next shift if you cleared this one.

## Where to start

1. [Install the game](02-Installing.md). It takes about two minutes.
2. Read [How to play](03-How-to-play.md) with the game open beside it.
3. Play the **Training day** first. It loads with a plan that has mistakes in it, shown in red, so you learn the controls by fixing them.

## Good to know

- The game runs entirely on your laptop. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Your progress is saved in your web browser. If you switch browser, you start again.
- You can play offline. The game looks slightly different without an internet connection because it cannot load its fonts, but it works the same.
