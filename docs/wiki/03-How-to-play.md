# How to play

Open the game and pick **Training day (the brief)** from the **Shift** menu at the top. It loads with a plan full of mistakes, so it is the best place to learn.

## The screen

| Part | Where | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Score strip | Across the top | Your score now, the house planner's score, and the scores for silver and gold |
| Notices | Under the score strip, in orange | What is different about this shift: a closed stand, fewer desks, late aircraft |
| Departures board | Left, dark panel | Every flight, its time, stand, gate and status. Red status means a problem |
| Flight editor | Right, top | The settings for the flight you have selected |
| Apron and gates | Right, below the editor | A timeline of every stand and gate, with each flight as a block. Red blocks clash |
| Usage charts | Bottom of the timeline | How many check-in desks (and, from shift 3, ground teams) are busy at each moment. Red means over the limit |

## Fixing a flight, step by step

1. **Click a flight** on the departures board. It turns yellow, and its details appear in the flight editor.
2. **Read the red lines** at the bottom of the editor. They say exactly what is wrong, for example "Stand S2 clashes with AL305".
3. **Pick a stand.** Click one of the stand buttons. Grey buttons are too small for this aircraft. Buttons with a red dashed edge would clash with another flight.
4. **Pick a gate** the same way.
5. **Set the desks** with the **−** and **+** buttons. The note beside them tells you when check-in would finish and the fewest desks that would get everyone through.
6. **Add delay if you must** with **+1** or **+5**. Delay moves the flight later, which frees up the stand and gate before it, but costs points every minute.
7. When the editor says **Good to go**, the flight is fixed. Move to the next red one.

You can also click straight onto a row of the timeline to put the selected flight on that stand or gate.

## Moving flights by dragging

On the **Apron and gates** timeline, each flight is a bar.

- **Drag a bar left or right** to change its delay. The bar shows the new delay as you drag, for example "BR237 +12".
- **Drag a bar up or down** onto another stand or gate row to move it there. The row lights up when you are over a row it can go to.
- **Click a bar** without dragging to select that flight.

## Running the shift

1. When the board shows no red, press **Start the shift** (yellow button, top right).
2. The clock starts at 06:30. A dark bar appears at the top showing the time. **Fast forward ×4** speeds it up.
3. **Disruptions are announced as they happen**: a late inbound aircraft, a stand or gate going out of use, or extra passengers for a flight. The bar turns yellow, the clock stops and a countdown starts.
4. **Re-plan before the countdown ends.** The affected flight is selected for you. Fix anything that has turned red, then press **Resume shift**. If the countdown runs out, the shift carries on as it is.
5. Once a flight's aircraft is on stand you can only hold it with more delay. Once its check-in opens, its desk count is fixed. Once it departs, its result is final.
6. When the last flight has gone, the **Shift debrief** appears: your score and medal, the three flights that cost you most points and why, and one thing to do differently next time.

The **Shift log** under the departures board lists every disruption, departure and cancellation as it happens.

Press **Replan this shift** in the debrief to go back to your plan and try the same shift again.

## The other buttons

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Show house plan** | Training day only. Fills in the house planner's plan so you can see what you are trying to beat |
| **New wave** | Deals a different set of flights at the same shift. Use it if you are stuck or want fresh practice |
| **Clear plan** | Empties every setting on this wave so you can start again |
| **Abandon shift** | During a shift, stops it and takes you back to planning |
| **Rules and scoring** | Opens the full rules at the bottom of the page |

Your plan saves itself as you go. You can close the browser and come back to it.
